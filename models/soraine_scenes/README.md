# Soraine 3D models (no code)

Raw models exported from the Soraine "Flame in Freefall" 3D site as binary glTF (`.glb`). Units are metres, Y is up.
They open in Blender (File > Import > glTF 2.0), three.js (GLTFLoader) or any glTF viewer.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `props/stove.glb` | Retro cream gas stove with burner, dials, backsplash and two candles |
| `props/pan.glb`, `props/egg.glb` | Frying pan; fried egg |
| `props/canister.glb` | Gas canister with valve wheel and hose |
| `props/bottle.glb`, `props/mug.glb`, `props/rocket.glb` | Oil bottle; enamel mug; toy rocket |
| `props/galley-props-arranged.glb` | All seven props in their scene positions |
| `scenes/earth-kitchen-room.glb` | Kitchen room: walls, tiles, counter, hood, fridge, shelf, window, table, stool, lamp |
| `scenes/moon-surface.glb` | Curved lunar ground with craters, rocks, pebbles, plant and loose utensils |
| `scenes/mars-surface.glb` | Curved Martian ground with mesas, dunes, rocks, pebbles, plant and loose utensils |

Notes
- Geometry and flat material colours only. Lights, glow sprites, dust, stars, sky, the Earth globe and photo textures are not included; the Moon and Mars ground carry vertex colours instead of textures.
- The models were built in code with AI assistance (Claude), directed by Ethan. The Moon and Mars landforms start from Tony's Blender terrains (`models/planet_surfaces`). They are stylised concept models, not to scale and not measured data.
